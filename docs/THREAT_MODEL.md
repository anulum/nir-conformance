# Data and authority boundaries

## Present tooling

Maintained source, selected executable files and the locked tooling environment are inputs to repository controls. Received descriptors and inventories cannot select policy or acquire execution authority. Checks inspect bounded local scopes and report fixed diagnostics. Byte identity differs from interpretation and qualification.

An inventory is not a concurrent filesystem snapshot or power-failure durability guarantee. Native children use finite argument vectors and direct-child deadlines; completed-output limits are not live volume ceilings. Foundation qualification is in progress.

## Future conformance sources

Graphs, suites, profiles, traces and certificates are supplied data. They do not grant framework installation, execution, signing, equipment control, provider access, spending or publication authority. Actual pinned adapters, source interpretation and independent reference admission precede use.

Preserve all required primitives, lanes, corners, version identities and declared support. No silent favourable subset, duplicated golden, missing event treated as tolerance success or framework exemption is admissible. A minimality claim retains its actual search scope.

Unknown numerical semantics, independent lineage or current trust cannot become positive eligibility. Shared evidence, external frameworks and operational recovery remain separate unimplemented boundaries; no scientific, isolation or durability guarantee is claimed here.
